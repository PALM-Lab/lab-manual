# Lab Manual for the PALM Lab

This is a guide for members and collaborators of the **P**erception, **A**ttention, **L**earning and **M**emory **Lab** at Adelaide University, run by Dr William Ngiam. The PALM Lab is a computational cognitive psychology and neuroscience lab researching how we information is represented in mind during visual cognition. You can find out more about our research and team on [our lab website](https://palm-lab.github.io).

This lab manual is mainly written for prospective or current members of the PALM Lab, but is shared publicly as a potentially helpful resource for conducting research and/or writing a lab manual. Each chapter contains guidelines, policies, and useful resources for how the lab conducts our research. It is a continual work in progress, as the way we conduct our scientific research evolves. Please feel free to share your feedback by [contacting Will via email](mailto:william.ngiam@adelaide.edu.au).
